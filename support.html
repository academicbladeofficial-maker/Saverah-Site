Reporting Inappropriate Content

UGC Safety & Content Takedown Policy:

Saverah takes community safety seriously. While our self-hosted TensorFlow.js/NSFWJS scanner pre-screens public submissions, community reporting is vital. If you observe any content violating our Prohibited Content Guidelines (such as pornography, hate speech, or harassment):

  1. In-App Report: Tap the options menu (⋮) on any public post or profile and select Report.
  2. Direct Support Email: Alternatively, email support@saverah.com with the subject line URGENT: Content Report, including the username, post link or screenshot, and the nature of the violation.

Reported items are reviewed by human administrators promptly. Confirmed violations result in immediate content removal and appropriate sanctions on the offending account.

Account & Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and global privacy laws, you can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

Open Saverah → Navigate to Settings → Select Account → Tap Delete Account. This immediately invokes our automated deletion service (account_deletion_service.js) to wipe your media, database rows, and credentials instantly.

Option 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, send an email to support@saverah.com with the subject Account Deletion Request from your registered email address. We will verify your identity and execute the deletion protocol within 48 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does private archival storage work?

When you designate a folder or item as "Private" in Saverah, it is stored in secure archival storage protected by database Row-Level Security (RLS). It is never indexed on our public discovery feed and cannot be searched or viewed by anyone other than yourself and members you explicitly invite.

Does Saverah track me or sell my information?

No. Saverah contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero data trackers, and never sells, rents, or monetizes personal data or uploaded media to data brokers or advertisers.

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen of the mobile application, select "Forgot Password" to receive a secure password reset link via Supabase Auth to your registered email address.

What file formats are supported for cultural documentation?

Saverah supports standard photo formats (JPEG, PNG, WebP) and video formats (MP4, WebM, MOV) up to our standard media size limits.
